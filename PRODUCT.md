# Yong funding tracker

A small public GitHub Pages site for Yong Chen and the Penn Computing, Inference, and Learning Lab. Researchers use it to compare funding fit, check submission dates and conditions, and see what changed in the latest biweekly review.

The local Excel workbook is the database. The website is read-only and publishes an explicit allowlist of opportunity research and ranking fields. Internal ownership, decisions, internal dates and user notes remain local. Official sponsor links support factual claims; project concepts and overlap assessments are labeled as judgments. Existing awards are research context, not pending applications.

## Surface brief

Mode: Operate. One responsive page with search, availability and funder filters, ranking/date sorting, expandable source-linked details, latest-review changes, and ranking methodology. Default view shows open and upcoming calls. Rank each group separately. Baseline means the first snapshot, not newly announced calls.

Visual authority: the user's Ividence funding dashboard, its Funding tracker and Funding opportunity Search tasks, and the Ividence portal DESIGN.md. Use paper surfaces, navy text/header, teal links, lime accents, thin dividers, a compact funding table and progressively disclosed evidence. Use the established Segoe UI/Avenir sans and Georgia editorial headings. On mobile, show each table record as a legible stacked entry.

Publish at howardchanth/yong_funding_tracker. The existing biweekly task reviews funding, updates Excel, exports public data, publishes the site, and verifies the live data version.
